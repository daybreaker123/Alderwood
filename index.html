Who we work with

Alderwood works with established companies, typically valued between $10 and $50 million. Industry is secondary. These companies already run on a set of business systems, and people on the team still carry work between those systems by hand.

The introductory conversation is a fit check. We are looking for work that is repeated, expensive in time or money, and realistic to automate with the software you already have.

  • Your team repeats a process often enough that people can describe the steps from memory.
  • Staff time goes to re-typing information, following up on approvals, or rebuilding a report that already exists somewhere in the company.
  • The business already runs on named software, and the slow part is the gap between those applications.
  • You want the people who do the work involved when a system goes live, and someone available after that first week.

Services

The analysis shows where to work. From there we plan and implement what that process needs. A project might be one automation, or an agent, a few integrations, and time with the team. Each one is specific to your workflows and to the applications already in place.

  1. Company analysis Free

    We study the company and interview employees to locate where the most time and money are wasted, and to name the processes AI can automate. You receive a written analysis: how the work moves, what people closest to it describe, where time and cost concentrate, and a first project with the applications it would connect to. There is no fee. It comes after the introductory conversation.

  2. AI agents

    For a bounded task, an agent can gather the inputs, follow rules you have approved, and prepare the next step. When a case needs a person, it is routed to someone on your team with the context attached.

  3. Workflow automations

    We automate the repeatable path around a process: intake, routing, record updates, reminders, and the handoff back to your staff.

  4. Integrations

    We connect the new workflow to the business software you already use, so people keep working in familiar applications and the records stay in sync.

  5. Adoption and maintenance

    We train the team that will use the system, document how it runs, and adjust it after the first weeks of real work. We can stay on to maintain it as your process, volume, or software changes.

About Alderwood

Alderwood is an AI consulting and implementation practice. Clients use us as a practical partner from the first look at a workflow through the weeks after a system is in operation.

The free analysis is the foundation. We scope a project against a real workflow, and we do that before proposing software. During the analysis we sit with employees, because the delay in a process is often obvious to the person who handles it and easy to miss in a management summary.

Value, for us, is specific to the process. We look at time spent, delays, rework, and whether the team is using what we built. We talk about results at that level, once they can be observed in the operation.
